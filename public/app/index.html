Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Originyx Client Portal?
The Originyx Client Portal (https://app.originyx.in) is the unified workspace where businesses discover, evaluate, license, and manage Originyx operational AI products and systems.

What is the core operating philosophy of Originyx?
Speed is the ultimate competitive advantage. Modern businesses lose time through disconnected systems, delayed follow-ups, and repetitive manual tasks. Originyx engineers practical software to reduce these operational delays.

Can Originyx platforms be deployed into private VPCs?
Yes. Enterprise deployments support private VPC isolation on AWS, GCP, or Azure with zero external data egress and dedicated SLAs.
