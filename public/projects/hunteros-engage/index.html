What Is HunterOS Engage by Originyx?

HunterOS Engage is Vertical Revenue Infrastructure that Originyx configures around your industry, sales process, and existing systems. It helps manage the routine work of checking enquiries, collecting customer details, qualifying interest, following up, and recording activity—so your team can focus on relationships and decisions.

  • Handle routine enquiry work: Check incoming enquiries, collect relevant details, and keep each request moving according to your process.
  • Remember customer conversations: Keep relevant history and use previous responses to guide the next follow-up.
  • Follow up with care: Schedule customer-specific follow-ups and pause or adjust them when a customer responds, based on configured rules.
  • Reduce manual CRM work: Help update customer details, conversation history, lead stages, and sales activities in supported connected systems.
  • Fit your business: Originyx configures the workflows and supported connections for your industry and existing systems.

How Does HunterOS Engage Differ from Generic AI SDR Tools?

HunterOS Engage is an operational system configured for your business, not a replacement for your CRM or sales team. It takes on repetitive revenue work around your existing tools, helping employees spend less time entering data and chasing enquiries and more time helping potential customers.

A revenue system built around your process

HunterOS Engage helps manage incoming enquiries, qualification, follow-ups, and CRM updates. Originyx configures its workflows for your industry's requirements and connects supported systems, so your existing CRM remains part of the process.

Follow-ups that remember the conversation

Follow-up is more than sending the same message again. HunterOS Engage can use conversation history to tailor the next step, schedule follow-ups, and stop or adjust the cadence when a customer responds. A plan of 8–12 touches may suit some journeys, depending on customer responses and your business rules.

Less time spent maintaining customer records

Sales teams may spend 10–25 hours a week on repetitive research, data entry, conversation notes, lead status updates, and reminders. HunterOS Engage can automate configured parts of this work, giving employees more time for customer relationships. The amount saved depends on the team's current workload and setup.

How Does the 3-Layer Vertical Revenue Architecture Work?

HunterOS Engage combines a reusable revenue-work foundation, workflows configured for your industry, and an Originyx integration layer. It is designed to work with supported parts of your current business systems, including your existing CRM.

Layer 1: Core Revenue Engine

Core Engine

The core responds to important business events, manages customer conversations, remembers previous interactions, and coordinates qualification and follow-ups.

  • Qualification: Checks whether an enquiry matches your business requirements.
  • Conversation history: Remembers relevant customer interactions to guide future steps.
  • Team handoff: Routes questions or opportunities to a person when your workflow calls for it.

Layer 2: Industry-Specific Capabilities

Domain Specialization

Originyx configures workflows to reflect the needs, customer questions, and qualification rules of your industry:

  • Real Estate: Collects buyer needs, budget, and preferred area for a team follow-up.
  • Logistics & Supply Chain: Organizes quote requests, shipment needs, and relevant handoffs.
  • High-Ticket B2B Sales: Checks business fit, buying needs, and decision-making details.

Layer 3: Originyx Integration

Integration Layer

Originyx maps the agreed workflow to your existing CRM and supported communication channels. Connections depend on account access and implementation requirements:

  • CRM connections: Potential systems include HubSpot, Zoho CRM, LeadSquared, and Salesforce, subject to supported access and configuration.
  • Communication channels: WhatsApp Business, email, and voice can be considered where the required connection is available; LinkedIn availability must be confirmed for each implementation.
  • Consistent records: Configured updates can reduce duplicate entry and help keep connected customer information in step.

How does HunterOS Engage prevent AI hallucinations during sales conversations?

HunterOS Engage prevents AI hallucinations through a deterministic finite-state machine (FSM) architecture integrated with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). Conversational responses are strictly constrained to approved product documentation, pricing rubrics, and compliance guidelines. When a prospect raises queries outside verified boundaries, the system executes an automated fallback that routes the thread to human operators with complete conversational context rather than guessing.

How does HunterOS Engage eliminate manual CRM workload for SME sales teams?

Sales teams can spend 10–25 hours a week on repetitive tasks such as entering customer details, recording conversations, updating lead stages, and chasing unanswered enquiries. HunterOS Engage can automate configured parts of this work and flag opportunities for a person to handle. Sales reps get more time for customer conversations; founders gain clearer activity visibility with less manual reporting; CRM and operations teams spend less time maintaining records and spreadsheets. Time saved depends on your setup.
